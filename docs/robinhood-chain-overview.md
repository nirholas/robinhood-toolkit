# Robinhood Chain — Overview

Every figure below is sourced from [`reports/chain-facts.json`](../reports/chain-facts.json),
regenerated by [`scripts/chain-facts.mjs`](../scripts/chain-facts.mjs). Values
observed at `2026-07-21T06:16:34Z`. Live market and cadence numbers are point-in-time
observations, not constants — rerun the prober before quoting them.

## What this chain is

Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit rollup running on the Nitro stack. It uses
ETH for gas, produces sub-second blocks, and settles to Ethereum with data
availability provided by Ethereum blobs. The sequencer and proposer are operated
by Robinhood. In one line: an L2 rollup, not a standalone L1, and not a Geth
chain — the client identifies itself as a Nitro build.

## A balance here is not a brokerage balance

Self-custody wallet services run through Robinhood Non-Custodial Ltd (Cayman
Islands), a separate entity from Robinhood Financial LLC and Robinhood Crypto
LLC. An on-chain balance on this network is not a brokerage balance.

Both halves are true and both matter. Your Robinhood app balance and your
on-chain balance are **not the same pool of funds** — moving one does not move
the other. They are also **not unrelated products** — this chain is Robinhood
infrastructure operated by a Robinhood entity. Do not describe Robinhood Chain
as "Robinhood's blockchain for trading stocks in the app"; the on-chain
environment and the brokerage are distinct entities with distinct balances, and
conflating them is the most common error in third-party writeups.

Mainnet and testnet are **not configured alike**. Testnet runs slower blocks and
a flat gas price; its numbers do not transfer to mainnet. Gas price is a live
market value — treat mainnet's ~0.058 gwei as a data point, not a constant.
Block cadence is measured empirically over a 1000-block window because Nitro
timestamps have one-second granularity and a small sample cannot resolve
sub-second blocks.

## Provenance

- **Stack:** Arbitrum Orbit chain on the Nitro stack. Settles to Ethereum. Data
  availability via Ethereum blobs.
- **Operator:** Robinhood runs both the sequencer and the proposer. L2BEAT
  classifies the risk profile as "Other" because fewer than five external actors
  can submit fraud challenges.
- **Timeline:** Mainnet live 2026-07-01. Testnet running since 2026-02-10.
- **Docs root:** <https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/>

## Reproduce

```sh
node scripts/chain-facts.mjs
# writes reports/chain-facts.json and prints a summary table

# dependency-free spot check:
curl -s https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_chainId","params":[]}'
# => {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"result":"0x1237"}
```

Cross-check the head block against <https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com>; the
two should agree within a few seconds of drift.
